Who We Are

A Craft Supply Factory in the World’s Small-Commodity Capital

Yiwu Sola Craft Co., Ltd. — registered 2016, workshop and warehouse in Yiwu, Zhejiang, China.

We run a 1,000 m² workshop with two production lines making polymer clay slices, resin charms, acrylic beads, sequins and craft fillers — 6,500+ live SKUs and roughly 100 new designs a year. Every batch is fully inspected with raw-material traceability; the factory is BSCI-audited and TüV Rheinland onsite-verified, holding a 4.8/5 rating across 300+ wholesale reviews.
