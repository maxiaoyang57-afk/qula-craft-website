How to Make a Beaded Bag Charm with a Heart Bead

Make a short clip-on charm with one acrylic heart, a few accent beads and doubled cord. Start by checking that both cord ends pass through every bead, then test the stopper knot and clip on the bag you plan to use.

November 12, 2026 · Qula Craft

What You Need

  • One focal heart bead and three accent beads with through-holes.
  • One length of flexible braided craft cord. Leave extra length for the knot and tails until the sample is finished.
  • One swivel clip or lobster clasp with an attachment loop for the cord, plus scissors and a ruler.

View the YA425 heart bead for a focal bead reference. The 2.5 mm dimension is the listed hole on that SKU. Test both ends of your chosen cord together through the actual bead and check the holes on your accents separately. If you are considering Qula's CDK5001-2 ring, chain and clasp assembly, confirm the attachment loop and clasp opening on a sample. The cord and beads are sourced separately.

How to Make the Charm

  1. Lay out the parts. Try two accents, the heart, then one accent. Thread a sample of the doubled cord through each bead before cutting your final length.
  2. Loop the cord onto the hardware. Fold the cord in half. Push the folded loop through the clip's attachment eye, pass both loose ends through the loop and pull the hitch snug. Use the fixed attachment eye so the clasp can still open.
  3. String and finish. Feed both loose ends through each bead in order. Tie the two tails together as one bundle in an overhand stopper knot below the final bead. Pull it snug and check that the knot cannot slip into the hole. If it can, change the cord or bottom bead and retest before trimming the tails.
  4. Check how it hangs. Attach the charm to the bag's ring. Measure from the top of the clip to the lowest part of the assembly, open the zipper or flap, and give the charm a gentle hand tug. Check for slipping cord, turning beads or a gate that does not close fully.

Unsure how long the charm should be? Watch the 50-second bag charm size guide to compare the drop on a tote, shoulder bag and crossbody before you finalize the cord length.

Plan a Small Run of 20 Charms

Using the layout above, allow 20 focal beads, 60 accent beads and 20 clips for 20 finished charms. With enough spare parts for two more complete charms, plan for 22 focal beads, 66 accents and 22 clips. Use the cut length from your approved sample to calculate the cord order.

For a bulk or sample inquiry, share a photo of the layout, your preferred bead colors, finished drop, clip style and quantity. Specify whether you need loose components or finished assemblies so the quote covers the right parts.
