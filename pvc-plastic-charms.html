FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask About This Line

What are PVC food charms used for?

Decoden cream-phone cases, dollhouse and miniature scenes, slime centerpieces and craft kits. Soft PVC cuts and glues easily, and survives handling better than brittle materials.

How realistic are the fruit slices?

Photo-realistic printing on both faces — tangerine, pitaya, strawberry, banana and pineapple slices are regularly used in fake-drink crafts and food photography props.

Should I choose PVC or resin charms?

PVC for soft, flexible, lightweight pieces at lower unit cost; resin for glossy rigid charms with fine 3D detail. Many buyers order both in one shipment — same carton, one freight bill.
