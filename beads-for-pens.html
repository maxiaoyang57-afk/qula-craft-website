FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask About This Line

What are beadable pen beads?

Large-hole focal beads that slide onto beadable pen shafts — the pens sold as blanks for craft assembly. One pen typically takes 1 focal bead plus spacers, so a 100pcs lot decorates roughly 100 pens.

Do the beads fit standard beadable pens?

Yes — hole sizes match the common beadable pen shafts sold on wholesale platforms. If you use a specific pen blank, send its shaft diameter with the inquiry and we confirm fit before shipping.

Can I mix themes in one 100pcs lot?

Stock lots ship as listed (single theme or fixed assortment). Custom mixed lots — your ratio of animals, fruits and seasonal motifs — are a light customization we run routinely.
