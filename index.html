What Actually Differs

Same Sprinkles. Five Things That Aren’t.

Two bags of clay slices can look identical in a photo and behave completely differently in production. These are the five checks buyers run when a sample lands — and what we hold ourselves to.

CheckWhy buyers careHow to test it yourself
Slice thicknessUneven cuts read as cheap in a clear slime jar and clog nail-art tweezers.Spread a spoonful on white paper — thickness should look even without measuring.
ColorfastnessDye migrating out of a slice tints a whole batch of clear slime pink overnight.Soak in warm water and in your activator for 24h. Liquid must stay clear.
Crumb rateDust and broken slivers at the bottom of the bag are weight you paid for but can’t sell.Pour the bag out. A quality kilogram leaves minimal powder behind.
Mix ratio consistencyIf shape ratios drift between orders, your retail packs stop looking like the same product.Count shapes per 100g against the written spec (we lock yours at ±10%).
OdourA strong plastic smell means rushed curing — it transfers into slime and kids’ kits.Open a fresh bag indoors. Properly cured polymer clay is near-odourless.

Every batch passes these checks before carton sealing — see the full QC chain or read the bulk buying guide.

Before You Order

Purchasing Questions, Answered Up Front

The commercial details buyers ask before a first order — MOQ, payment, freight and sampling.

What is the minimum order?

Stock items start at one bag — typically 100pcs or 500g depending on the item. You can mix several stock SKUs in one shipment to hit a workable freight volume. Custom shapes and private label packs carry design-based minimums confirmed per project.

How is payment handled?

Orders are confirmed with a proforma invoice covering product, quantity, packaging, price, lead time and shipping terms. Payment terms are agreed per order — tell us your preferred method in the inquiry and our sales team will confirm what works for your market.

Who pays freight, and how much is it?

Freight depends on weight, volume and destination, so it is quoted separately from goods. Craft supplies are light but bulky once packed, so carton dimensions matter more than weight — we provide carton data with every quote so you can compare door-to-door rates.

Can I get samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Stock samples ship quickly; custom projects always include a pre-production sample so shape, colour, mix ratio and packaging are confirmed before mass production.
