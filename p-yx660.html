Detailed Product Description

Developed for slime brands, tumbler makers, nail suppliers and winter craft wholesalers, this wholesale snowflake clay slice and pearl mix combines snowflakes with pearl and candy-sprinkle accents with a soft matte clay texture with pearl highlights. The blue, pink, white and customizable soft winter palettes palette makes it easy to build a coordinated soft winter snowflake collection range. It can be supplied as a single design, a mixed motif set or a buyer-specific color assortment for slime, snow-globe tumblers, resin crafts, shaker ornaments, winter nails and pastel Christmas kits. For custom programs, final dimensions, mix ratios, finish, packaging and carton details are confirmed through an approved sample before bulk production.

Particle size and thickness; color palette and color ratio; motif/shape assortment; glitter, pearl or rhinestone blend; unit weight; inner-bag count; retail pouch, header card, barcode and private-label carton. Custom blends are produced after sample confirmation.
