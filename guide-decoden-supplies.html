Decoden Supplies: What Actually Goes on a Phone Case

Updated 22 October 2026 · by the Qula Craft Sourcing Team — the people who quote and pack these lines every week.

Short answer: decoden needs flatback pieces that glue flush, in a deliberate mix of sizes — a few large focal pieces, more mid-size, and a scatter of small fillers. Browse the resin charm catalogue, then confirm the flatback style, dimensions, pack count and minimum order for each selected SKU. The catalogue includes several attachment and backing styles; not every charm is suitable for a phone case.

1. Check the reverse side before choosing a design

For glue-on decoden, start with a flat reverse side and enough contact area for your case and adhesive. A front-view photograph does not show the backing, thickness or attachment hardware. Request a reverse-side photo for each selected SKU, then test a sample on your intended case material.

The word charm can describe a drilled piece, a looped pendant or a flatback component. Specify flatback cabochon for glue-on use, and confirm whether a hole or loop is present. Do not assume that every design in the resin charm catalogue has the same backing.

2. Plan the layout around your actual phone case

Measure the usable decoration area, leaving the camera, buttons, ports and edges clear. Arrange a paper outline or sample layout before deciding quantities. Larger focal pieces, smaller supporting pieces and fillers can create a balanced layout, but the number needed depends on the case model, piece dimensions and spacing.

4. Review samples and repeat-order details

Ask for current-batch photos or samples when appearance matters. Compare the selected SKU with the agreed reference: colour, surface finish, moulded detail, dimensions and backing. Record the accepted sample and packing details so a repeat order has a clear reference.

  • Check fit: place samples on the actual case, including near the camera opening and edges.
  • Check attachment: follow your adhesive supplier's instructions and assess the sample after curing. A flat backing alone does not guarantee a durable bond.
  • Check the batch: confirm which colours, finishes and dimensions are covered by the quotation rather than assuming all variants are identical.

5. Combine resin pieces and clay slices deliberately

Resin shapes can serve as focal or supporting decorations. Polymer clay slices can add small patterns and fill gaps when their actual dimensions suit the layout. Check the selected pieces' thickness, backing and finish; material names alone do not confirm compatibility with your adhesive or case.

Food-shaped decorations are not edible. Review the intended user, small-parts risks and destination-market requirements. If documentation is needed, confirm its exact product and batch scope rather than assuming that one report covers every SKU. See the non-food safety labeling guide for questions to discuss.

6. Build a quote-ready starter assortment

Start with one theme and a trial layout. Send the chosen SKUs, colours, required quantities and phone-case model or usable decoration dimensions. Include reverse-side and thickness requirements, packing preferences and any documentation questions.

Confirm the minimum order, pack count and sample arrangements for each SKU in the quotation. Source the base and adhesive according to the needs of your project, and test the complete combination before bulk assembly.

Decoden buying questions

Does every resin charm have a flat back?

No. Charm listings can include different backing and attachment styles. Request a reverse-side photo and confirm the backing for the exact SKU before choosing it for glue-on decoden.

How many pieces do I need for one phone case?

There is no fixed count. Measure the usable case area, arrange a trial layout with the actual piece dimensions, and count each SKU while keeping the camera, buttons and ports clear.

What should I send for a decoden supply quotation?

Send the selected SKUs, colours, quantities, case model or usable decoration dimensions, backing requirements and packing preferences. Confirm missing dimensions, minimum orders and sample arrangements before ordering.
