Start with the usable surface of your phone case

Mark the camera opening, edges, buttons and areas your hand touches. Lay out the actual decorations before applying adhesive. A flat reverse side can provide a useful bonding area, but the name “charm” does not tell you the backing shape, thickness or attachment method.

Ask for a reverse-side photograph for each shortlisted SKU. Check that the component sits as intended on the case and leaves the openings clear. A decorative front photograph is useful for color and theme; it cannot establish adhesive performance or the exact clearance above a camera lens.

Compare a few actual sizes, then build your layout

The photographed references above give three different starting points: RW967 lists 35 × 31 × 8 mm, RW474 lists 24 × 15 mm, and RW26460 lists 14 × 13 × 9 mm. These are product references, not a prescribed focal, middle and filler set. A suitable combination depends on the usable case area and the sample pieces you select.

The RW4252 jelly-bean decoration has a verified 12 × 22 mm size reference on its product page. Compare that footprint with your plan and request its thickness and reverse-side view if those affect the fit. The separate candy decoden layout guide offers a way to plan the composition.

Photograph a dry layout with the SKU beside each position. Count how many pieces of each SKU it uses. Leave space around raised components and check how the case rests on a table. Adjust the layout before calculating an order.

Test the decoration, adhesive and base together

Follow the adhesive supplier's instructions for the actual materials. Trial the selected decoration on the case surface and any cream-style base you intend to use. Review contact, appearance and handling after the specified curing period. A successful assembly with one adhesive or case does not establish the same result with another.

Decide whether the assembled item needs a packing insert or extra clearance above raised decorations. Place the sample in the intended packaging and check that the lid or outer carton does not press on the focal pieces. Assembly tools, case blanks and adhesive are separate requirements unless explicitly included in the quotation.

Work from the layout count to a clear pack enquiry

Multiply your planned number of cases by the number of each selected SKU in the approved layout. For example, 40 cases using two pieces of one SKU require 80 pieces of that SKU before any agreed assembly allowance. This is an example calculation, not a proposed order or minimum.

RW967, RW474 and RW26460 each list a 100-piece reference bag, with packing details confirmed by inquiry. A bag quantity does not establish the minimum order. Ask whether the selected colors are supplied mixed or separately and whether a mixed bag permits individual design selection.

Approve what matters for a repeat order

Keep a front and reverse photograph, the selected dimensions and the approved color reference. Compare the next sample for shape, edges, surface finish and the details important to your layout. Ask for clarification if a product image and the written assortment description appear different.

For a fruit collection, RW26460 mini fruit decorations provide a starting reference; confirm the dimensions of the specific fruit designs selected. For a candy collection, RW474 candy decorations show the wrapper-style motif. Neither image guarantees a fixed mix ratio or a finished phone-case kit.

Request a quotation with the layout attached

Send the selected SKUs, quantity per SKU, color or assortment preference, reverse-side requirements, packaging and destination. Include the trial layout if you want help comparing component sizes. Ask about sample availability, minimums and dispatch dates for that precise selection.

Browse resin decorations and cabochons for other motifs, or polymer clay slices for smaller decorative patterns. Product use, documentation and any age or market requirements need to be assessed for the finished item you plan to sell.
