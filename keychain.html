FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask About This Line

Are keychains sold finished or as parts?

The catalog includes finished charm keychains and screw-lock wire key rings. Check the selected SKU and its product image to confirm the format and included components.

What lot sizes do keychains ship in?

Pack sizes vary by SKU. The listed Christmas charm keychains and CDK080 have a 100-piece bag reference. Confirm your assortment, sample packing and minimum order quantity by inquiry.

Can you build a custom keychain theme?

Yes — pick charms from the resin catalog and we assemble them as keychains, or develop exclusive shapes. Summer and Christmas custom programs should start one season early.
