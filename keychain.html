FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask About This Line

Are keychains sold finished or as parts?

Finished — resin charm, jump ring and key ring assembled. Out of the bag and onto a display hook; no assembly labor on your side.

What lot sizes do keychains ship in?

Most stock designs come in 10pcs lots with 1pc samples available, so a display of 10 designs costs one small test order before you commit deeper.

Can you build a custom keychain theme?

Yes — pick charms from the resin catalog and we assemble them as keychains, or develop exclusive shapes. Summer and Christmas custom programs should start one season early.
