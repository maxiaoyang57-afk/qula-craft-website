FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask About This Line

What bag sizes do slime charm packs come in?

Stock packs run from 30g trial bags through small themed bags to 500g and 1000g bulk. Most slime shops start with a spread of themed small bags, then reorder winners in bulk weight.

Are the packs themed or randomly mixed?

Both. Themed packs (Halloween, Christmas, ocean, snack food, floral) keep a fixed motif set; assorted packs mix shapes for variety jars. Themed packs photograph better for listings.

Can I combine charm packs with plain clay sprinkles in one order?

Yes — most buyers pair 70% plain sprinkle mixes with 30% charm packs so every jar gets texture plus one centerpiece. One shipment, one carton, matched color stories.
