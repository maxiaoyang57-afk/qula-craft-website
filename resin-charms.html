Wholesale Buying Guide

Choose Resin Charms by Back Type and End Use

Compare the attachment method first, then confirm size, finish and packing for each SKU. A flatback for glue-on decoration is not interchangeable with a drilled charm for a jump ring.

Phone Cases, Hair Clips and Decoden

Start with flatback designs and check the back surface, height and adhesive compatibility on a sample. For food-themed projects, compare RW22372 chocolate chip cookie cabochons and RW4252 jelly bean charms; confirm each SKU's dimensions and reverse side before ordering. The decoden supplies guide explains how to plan a coordinated assortment.

Slime Toppings and Mixed Craft Kits

Confirm the size and weight against your container or finished kit, and test samples with the adhesive or slime formula you use. Compare slime charms and mixes with polymer clay slices using the clay slices versus resin charms guide.

Send a Quote Request That Can Be Compared

Include the SKU, quantity, sales unit, color mix, back type, packing preference and destination. Ask whether the quoted price is per piece or per bag and how many pieces the bag contains. Review the sample and inspection checklist before confirming a bulk order, then request a resin charm quotation.

FAQ

Common Questions for This Product Category

MOQ, customization, safety and sampling — answered before you write to us.

What is the difference between flatback and drilled charms?

Flatbacks have a flat, glue-ready base for decoden, phone cases and hair clips. Drilled charms carry a hole for jump rings, keychains and jewelry. Most themes are available in both.

Which glue works with your resin charms?

Standard craft adhesives — E6000-type glue and UV resin both bond well to the flat base. The surface is degreased before packing, so no extra prep is needed.

Do resin charms yellow over time?

We use UV-stabilized resin for clear pieces; colored and opaque charms are unaffected in normal retail conditions. Avoid months of direct sunlight in displays, as with any resin product.

How accurate are charm sizes across a bag?

Cast charms of the same design hold tight size tolerance — differences you would need calipers to find. Mixed-theme bags state the size range per design on the spec sheet.

Can I build a custom charm theme set?

Yes — pick designs from stock molds for a custom assortment with your ratio, or develop exclusive shapes. Assortments are counted per bag (e.g. 100pcs, 8 designs) so retail packs stay consistent.
