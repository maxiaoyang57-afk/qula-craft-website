FAQ

Common Questions for This Product Category

MOQ, customization, safety and sampling — answered before you write to us.

What is the difference between flatback and drilled charms?

Flatbacks have a flat, glue-ready base for decoden, phone cases and hair clips. Drilled charms carry a hole for jump rings, keychains and jewelry. Most themes are available in both.

Which glue works with your resin charms?

Standard craft adhesives — E6000-type glue and UV resin both bond well to the flat base. The surface is degreased before packing, so no extra prep is needed.

Do resin charms yellow over time?

We use UV-stabilized resin for clear pieces; colored and opaque charms are unaffected in normal retail conditions. Avoid months of direct sunlight in displays, as with any resin product.

How accurate are charm sizes across a bag?

Cast charms of the same design hold tight size tolerance — differences you would need calipers to find. Mixed-theme bags state the size range per design on the spec sheet.

Can I build a custom charm theme set?

Yes — pick designs from stock molds for a custom assortment with your ratio, or develop exclusive shapes. Assortments are counted per bag (e.g. 100pcs, 8 designs) so retail packs stay consistent.
