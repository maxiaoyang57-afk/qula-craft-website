FAQ

Common Questions for This Product Category

MOQ, customization, safety and sampling — answered before you write to us.

How consistent is slice thickness within one bag?

Cane-cut slices hold a visibly even thickness across the bag. Ask us for a close-up video of a random handful of your chosen mix — we send one before you commit.

Will clay slices bleed color in slime or resin?

Properly cured slices do not. Every batch passes a colorfast check before shipping; for your own QC, soak a spoonful in warm water or activator solution for 24 hours — the liquid should stay clear.

What sizes should I order for my application?

1–3mm for nail art, 2–5mm for slime toppings and shaker crafts, 5–8mm chunky pieces for butter slime and decoden, 8–12mm for statement pieces and kit heroes.

How is a custom mix specified?

By shapes per 100g — for example, a fruit mix with six shapes in equal ratio ±10%. That written ratio is what makes your reorders look identical on the shelf.

What is the lead time for stock mixes vs custom shapes?

Stock mixes ship in 3–7 days. A new custom shape needs cane design, a sample round and production — plan 15–25 days before shipping, longer in the August–October peak.
