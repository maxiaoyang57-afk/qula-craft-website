FAQ

Common Questions for This Product Category

MOQ, customization, safety and sampling — answered before you write to us.

How consistent is slice thickness within one bag?

Thickness depends on the selected design and batch. Request close-up photos or samples of your chosen mix and confirm the required thickness and acceptable variation before bulk ordering.

Will clay slices bleed color in slime or resin?

Color performance depends on the selected slices and the finished product formulation. Request samples for your intended slime, resin or coating system and agree on any required colorfastness checks before bulk ordering.

What sizes should I order for my application?

Use the dimensions listed for the selected SKU and the scale of your finished project. Send your target size and intended use, such as nail art, slime decoration or a DIY kit, so the suitable designs and sizes can be confirmed.

How is a custom mix specified?

List the shapes, colors and target proportions, and state whether the ratio is by piece count or weight. Confirm the packing unit, acceptable variation and approved sample as the reference for the order and any reorders.

What is the lead time for stock mixes vs custom shapes?

Dispatch timing depends on the selected SKUs, quantity, current availability and any custom or packing work. Confirm the planned dispatch date in the quotation; shipping transit time is reviewed separately for the destination.
