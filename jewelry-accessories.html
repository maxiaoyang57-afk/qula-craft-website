FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask About This Line

Which findings do you stock?

Swivel lobster clasps, spring buckles, metal chain connectors, extension chains, stainless carabiners and key rings — the parts a charm or keychain business burns through weekly.

Do the metal colors match your charm lines?

That is the point of the range — pastel and candy coatings are matched to the same palettes as our charms and beads, so assembled products look designed, not sourced from three suppliers.

Can findings ship together with charms and beads?

Yes, and they should — combining findings with charms in one carton saves a separate freight bill and keeps color batches aligned in one order.
