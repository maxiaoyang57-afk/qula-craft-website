Detailed Product Description

Developed for miniature wholesalers, slime brands, gift-kit suppliers and holiday craft retailers, this wholesale mixed 3D Christmas miniatures combines snowman, Santa, Christmas tree and gift-box miniatures with a colorful detailed molded finish. The classic festive assortment with custom color themes available palette makes it easy to build a coordinated christmas miniature scene collection range. It can be supplied as a single design, a mixed motif set or a buyer-specific color assortment for snow globes, crystal balls, slime scenes, fairy gardens, dollhouses, party favors and Christmas DIY kits. For custom programs, final dimensions, mix ratios, finish, packaging and carton details are confirmed through an approved sample before bulk production.
