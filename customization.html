6-Step Custom Process

A Clear Path for Wholesale Buyers

Six steps from idea to delivery — you know exactly what to send and what happens next.

01

Inquiry & Idea

Send product use, reference image, shape, color, quantity and packaging need.

02

Design Proposal

Confirm feasible shape, size, mold, color theme and mix composition.

03

Sample Making

Prepare stock sample, mix sample or custom sample based on requirements.

04

Sample Approval

Approve sample, packaging, label, carton mark and final quote details.

05

Mass Production

Produce, sort, inspect and pack according to the confirmed standard.

06

Packing & Shipping

Export packing, shipment coordination and reorder information storage.
