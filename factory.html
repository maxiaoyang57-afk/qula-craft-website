Small Items Need Clear Standards

Color, count, size and packing method are locked in writing before production starts — that is what keeps reorders identical.

  • Sample confirmation before custom mass production
  • Color and theme sorting before packing
  • Weight/count check according to packaging requirement
  • Appearance check for obvious damage and defects
  • Carton mark and export packing check before shipment
