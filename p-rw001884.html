Detailed Product Description

Developed for gift wholesalers, car-accessory retailers, lifestyle stores and holiday promotional buyers, this wholesale 3D gingerbread character mini decorations combines cute gingerbread people designed for small seasonal displays with a detailed decorative finish suitable for gifting. The gingerbread brown with festive red, green and white accents palette makes it easy to build a coordinated nostalgic gingerbread christmas collection range. It can be supplied as a single design, a mixed motif set or a buyer-specific color assortment for desk décor, car dashboards, mirror ornaments, miniature displays, gift sets and Christmas promotional items. For custom programs, final dimensions, mix ratios, finish, packaging and carton details are confirmed through an approved sample before bulk production.

Product dimensions; color palette; motif assortment; glossy/matte/glitter finish; flatback, no-hole or hanging/stringing version where structurally feasible; pieces per pack; retail pouch, header card, barcode, logo label and export carton. New molds are subject to feasibility and sample approval.
