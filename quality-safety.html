Quality & Safety Notes for Non-Edible Craft Supplies

Non-food labeling, batch test reports and per-order QC records for slime, nail art and kids-craft supply chains.

Non-Edible Labeling

Polymer clay slices, resin charms, sequins and acrylic fillers are decorative craft supplies only. They should not be marketed as edible sprinkles.

Small Parts Warning

For small pieces used in kids craft or sensory kits, packaging should include appropriate small-part and age guidance based on buyer market requirements.

Batch Consistency

Before mass production, confirm shape, size range, color direction, mix ratio, packaging weight/count and acceptable variation.

FAQ

Safety Questions Buyers Often Ask

Batch test reports available on request: EN 71, ASTM F963, CPC, CPSC-CH-E1002, REACH, CE and RoHS — plus BSCI social-compliance audit and TüV Rheinland onsite verification.

Which certifications can you actually provide?

Batch test reports for EN 71 (parts 1–3), ASTM F963, CPC, CPSC-CH-E1002, REACH, CE and RoHS are available on request, plus BSCI social-compliance audit and TüV Rheinland onsite verification of the factory.

Can I get a test report for my specific order?

Yes — tell us your market (EU, US or both) and channel in the inquiry. Reports are issued per product batch, and testing lead time is planned into your production schedule.

How does batch traceability work?

Each production run carries a batch code linking raw materials, production date and inspection record. A complaint about one jar can be traced to one run — and answered with evidence.

What do you inspect before shipping?

Raw material review, color sorting, sample-standard comparison, count and weight check per pack, appearance inspection, carton packing check and a final pre-shipment verification — on every batch, not samples only.

How do you handle defects or disputes?

Report with photos and the batch code within the agreed window; confirmed issues are compensated on the next order or reshipped. The written spec sheet agreed before production is the reference standard.
