Buyer Guide

How to Source Polymer Clay Sprinkles in Bulk

Updated October 14, 2026 · by the Qula Craft sales team

Quick answer: Buy polymer clay sprinkles by a confirmed pack weight and quantity. Before ordering, agree on slice size, shape mix, sample performance and packaging. Compare the quoted unit with your planned retail fill weight. MOQ, availability and lead time depend on the item and any customization; confirm them in the quotation.

1. Match slice size to what you actually sell

Choose dimensions around the finished product and check the exact SKU with a sample. A photograph does not establish slice size, thickness or suitability for your application.

ApplicationMeasurements to confirmSample check
Nail art or small cavitiesSlice width and thicknessCheck the available surface or cavity and the intended finish.
Slime toppingsSlice dimensions and mix compositionTry the mix in your actual slime formula and review appearance and texture.
Shakers or tumblersLargest piece and thicknessCheck the opening, internal clearance and intended liquid or medium.
DIY kits or decodenDimensions of each componentCheck the layout, attachment method and packaging with a sample.

For mixed-size themes, specify the size range and shape ratio against an approved sample. For a seasonal example, review YX4075 St. Patrick’s Day polymer clay slices with shamrock and green hat motifs, then confirm the pack weight and dimensions for your application.

2. Do the gram math before you set prices

Confirm whether the quotation is per bag, pack or kilogram before comparing prices. The following are arithmetic examples using 1,000g of usable material, with no filling loss. These fill weights are planning inputs, not standard pack sizes or recommended doses.

Example fill weightComplete packs per 1kgMaterial remaining
10g1000g
15g6610g
20g500g
25g400g

Complete packs = usable material weight ÷ target fill weight, rounded down. For example, 1,000g ÷ 15g gives 66 complete packs and 10g left over. Check the filled volume with your own container and sample. Use a trial fill to estimate handling loss and any extra material needed to meet your agreed fill-weight tolerance. Net weight does not establish a fixed piece count.

3. Stock mix vs custom shape — different rules

Catalog mixes let you start from existing fruit, heart, star or holiday designs. Confirm current availability and compare a sample with your requirements. Custom shapes need a design review and sample approval; feasibility, minimum quantity and timing are confirmed for each project.

  • Catalog designs: confirm MOQ, available quantity and dispatch timing for the selected SKU.
  • Custom shape: agree on design, sample approval, production timing and minimum quantity before confirming the order.
  • Custom mix of catalog shapes: specify your preferred colors and ratios, then confirm component availability and the final sample. See customization options.

4. Agree on sample checks before ordering

  • Dimensions and thickness. Record the measurement range and sampling method for the selected SKU. Compare the sample with the space available in your finished product.
  • Color transfer and compatibility. Test the sample with your actual slime, resin, liquid or adhesive under its supplier's instructions. Agree on the observation period and acceptable result; a water soak alone does not establish suitability for every medium.
  • Dust and broken pieces. Agree how these will be measured and what level is acceptable. Use the trial result when estimating usable weight.
  • Mix composition. List required and excluded components, target proportions, whether proportions are measured by weight or count, and whether every pack must contain every design. Confirm feasible tolerances before approval.
  • Unexpected odor or surface changes. Record any change from the approved sample and ask the supplier to investigate before acceptance. Odor alone does not establish curing quality or material safety.
All polymer clay sprinkles are decorative and non-edible. If you sell into kids’ channels, read our safety labeling guide · Slime business starter supply checklist before printing packaging.

4b. Keep material claims specific to the selected product

Ask for the material description, finish and sample for the exact product code, especially when a mix includes several types of decoration. A pattern or product photograph does not establish resistance to heat, color transfer or compatibility with a resin system.

For resin inclusions, review a sample in the intended resin and follow the resin supplier's mixing and curing instructions. Confirm the finished appearance and performance before bulk production. Compare custom options using the quoted design, sampling, tooling, quantity and packing requirements; do not assume one material is always cheaper.

5. What to send for a fast, accurate quote

One complete message beats five rounds of back-and-forth. Include: application (slime / nail art / kits), size band, theme or reference photo, quantity in kg, packaging format (compare options here), private label needs, destination country and target date. FAQ

Questions buyers ask about this topic

How many retail units does 1kg of clay sprinkles make?

With no filling loss, 1,000g makes 100 complete packs at 10g, 66 at 15g with 10g left over, 50 at 20g, or 40 at 25g. These are calculation examples, not recommended fill weights. Estimate usable weight and filling loss from a trial with your selected mix and container.

What is a normal bulk order size for a first order?

Start with a sample and estimate the quantity from your planned fill weight and number of retail units. Confirm the minimum quantity for each SKU; custom shapes and mixes may have different minimums.

How long does a custom shape take versus stock?

Catalog designs depend on current availability. Custom shapes add design review and sample approval before production. Request the sampling, production and shipping schedule with your quotation.
