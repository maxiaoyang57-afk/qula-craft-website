Buyer Guide

How to Source Polymer Clay Sprinkles in Bulk

Updated July 6, 2026 · by the Qula Craft sales team

Quick answer: Buying polymer clay sprinkles in bulk means ordering by the kilogram, not the bottle. Confirm four things before paying: slice size range for your application, mix ratio per 100g, colorfastness, and how the kilograms convert into your retail units. Stock mixes ship in days with flexible MOQ; custom shapes need a new cane design and 2–4 weeks.

1. Match slice size to what you actually sell

“Sprinkles” covers everything from 1mm nail-art dots to 12mm statement pieces. Ordering the wrong size band is the most common first-order mistake — a nail studio can’t use 8mm fruit slices, and 2mm micro slices disappear inside slime.

Size bandBest forTypical packaging
1–3mm microNail art, resin fills, micro decoden10g bottles, compartment boxes
2–5mm standardSlime toppings, shaker crafts, tumblersBulk bags, 15–20g jars
5–8mm chunkyButter slime, decoden cream, party scatterBulk bags, wide-mouth jars
8–12mm statementPhone case centerpieces, kit hero piecesCounted packs, blister cards

Mixed-size themes (e.g. a fruit mix with 2mm dots and 6mm fruit slices) sell better in retail but must be specified as a ratio — see below.

2. Do the gram math before you set prices

Bulk clay sprinkles are quoted per kilogram. Your margin depends on how you split that kilogram into retail units:

Retail formatFill weightUnits per 1kg
Nail art bottle10g~100
Slime topping jar15g~65
Craft bag20–25g40–50
Slime jar topping (used in production)5–15g per 8oz slime70–200 slimes

Two practical notes: clay slices are light for their volume, so a 15g jar looks generously full — good for perceived value; and always order 3–5% overage for the slices that arrive chipped or fused, which is normal in transit.

3. Stock mix vs custom shape — different rules

Stock mixes (fruit, heart, star, holiday standards) already have canes made. MOQ is flexible, sampling is cheap, and repeat orders match earlier batches closely. Custom shapes require designing a new cane — your logo, a mascot, an unusual fruit. That means a design round, a physical sample, and a real minimum so the cane is worth cutting.

  • Stock: flexible MOQ, ships 3–7 days, ideal for testing themes.
  • Custom shape: design + sample 7–12 days, production 15–25 days, minimums confirmed per design complexity.
  • Custom mix of stock shapes (your ratio, your color story): the sweet spot — near-stock speed with a brand-exclusive look. See customization options.

4. The five quality checks that separate suppliers

  • Slice thickness variance. Good canes cut to a visibly even thickness; ask for a close-up video of a random handful.
  • Color bleed. Drop slices in warm water for 24 hours — dye should not tint the water. Critical for slime (activator solutions) and clear resin work.
  • Crumb rate. A quality kilogram has minimal dust and broken slivers at the bottom of the bag.
  • Mix ratio consistency. Specify shapes per 100g (e.g. “fruit mix: 6 shapes, equal ratio ±10%”) so reorders look identical on the shelf.
  • Smell. Properly cured polymer clay is near-odorless. A strong plastic smell means rushed curing.
All polymer clay sprinkles are decorative and non-edible. If you sell into kids’ channels, read our safety labeling guide · Slime business starter supply checklist before printing packaging.

4b. Why clay slices hold their pattern

The reason a clay slice survives being mixed into slime or set in resin is that the pattern is not printed on it. Polymer clay is a PVC-based modelling material cured at roughly 129–135 °C, and the designs are built as canes so the image runs right through the body of the slice. Cut a slice in half and the pattern is still there.

Two practical consequences: a scuffed slice still shows its design, and a slice that reaches you fully cured will not soften in the exothermic heat of a curing resin — which is exactly why these work as inclusions rather than only as surface decoration.

Compare that with cast resin, where each piece comes out of a mould and a flexible mould yields roughly 25 to 100 castings before fine detail degrades. One clay cane slices into a very large number of pieces; a resin mould wears out. That difference is the reason clay sprinkles price per gram and detailed resin charms price per piece — and why a custom clay colourway is usually far cheaper to originate than a custom moulded shape.

5. What to send for a fast, accurate quote

One complete message beats five rounds of back-and-forth. Include: application (slime / nail art / kits), size band, theme or reference photo, quantity in kg, packaging format (compare options here), private label needs, destination country and target date. FAQ

Questions buyers ask about this topic

How many retail units does 1kg of clay sprinkles make?

Roughly 100 bottles at 10g, 65 jars at 15g, or 40–50 bags at 20–25g. Slime toppings run 5–15g per 8oz jar, so 1kg covers about 70–200 finished slimes depending on how heavily you top.

What is a normal bulk order size for a first order?

Most first orders land between 5–20kg per mix for established sellers, or a sample pack plus 1–3kg per SKU for new brands testing themes. Stock mixes are flexible; custom shapes need larger commitments.

How long does a custom shape take versus stock?

Stock mixes typically ship in 3–7 days. A new custom shape needs cane design, sample confirmation and production — plan 15–25 days before shipping, longer in peak season (August–October).
