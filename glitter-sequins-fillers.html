FAQ

Common Questions for This Product Category

MOQ, customization, safety and sampling — answered before you write to us.

What sequin shapes and sizes do you stock?

Hearts, stars, rounds, moons, butterflies and holiday shapes from ~3mm to 25mm, in solid, metallic, iridescent and glow finishes — sold by weight in bulk bags.

What are fishbowl beads and how are they used?

Slightly flattened transparent acrylic beads that create the classic crunchy ‘fishbowl’ slime texture. Typical usage is a 1:3 to 1:1 bead-to-slime volume ratio depending on crunch level.

Do sequins float or sink in slime?

Standard PVC sequins are near-neutral and stay distributed through the slime body; heavier acrylic gems settle toward the base of the jar — mix both for depth.

Will glitter or sequins bleed color?

Quality PVC sequins and PET glitter are colorfast in slime and resin. Every batch passes the same soak check as our clay slices before shipping.

How is glitter packed for export?

By weight in sealed bulk bags (usually 500g or 1kg), or filled into retail bottles and shaker jars with your label. Light product, low freight impact — it packs flat.
