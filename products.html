How to Build a Better Product Line

A B2B craft supply website should guide buyers from product discovery to custom program planning.

1. Start with Core SKUs

Fruit slices, heart slices, kawaii resin charms and acrylic beads are easier for buyers to test quickly.

2. Add Seasonal Mixes

Holiday collections improve repeat purchases and give buyers a reason to source early.

3. Upgrade with Packaging

Private label bags, jars, boxes and SKU labels raise perceived value and make reordering easier.
