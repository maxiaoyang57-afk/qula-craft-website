Bag Charms for Purses: 3 Styling Ideas and a Size Guide

Watch three clear heart charms on a structured tan tote, an ivory shoulder bag and a compact dark brown crossbody.

Three Bag Charm Ideas by Bag Size

Bag styleReference bag sizeSuggested heart widthSuggested full drop
Structured tan tote25 × 25 × 12 cm (9.8 × 9.8 × 4.7 in), W × H × D4–5 cm (1.6–2.0 in)8–10 cm (3.1–3.9 in)
Ivory shoulder bag22–28 cm wide (8.7–11.0 in)4–5 cm (1.6–2.0 in)7–9 cm (2.8–3.5 in)
Dark brown crossbody16–22 cm wide (6.3–8.7 in)3–4 cm (1.2–1.6 in)6–8 cm (2.4–3.1 in)
  • Tan tote. Try the lime-green candy heart near the handle ring so it hangs over the front panel.
  • Ivory shoulder bag. Pair the blue star and mint ice cream design with the strap ring, then check how the charm sits when worn.
  • Dark brown crossbody. Clip the red strawberry heart at the side so the flap can open freely.

Measure the Drop and Check the Clasp

  1. Measure the heart across its widest point.
  2. Measure the full drop from the top of the clip or ring to the bottom of the assembled charm. Include the hardware in your measurement.
  3. Check the clasp opening against the thickest part of your bag ring. Clip it on and confirm the gate closes fully.
  4. Test it with the bag loaded. Open the zipper or flap and make sure the charm hangs clear of the opening and base. Use the size chart as a starting point.
