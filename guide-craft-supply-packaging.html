Best Packaging for DIY Craft Supplies: Bags, Bottles, Jars & Boxes

Updated October 16, 2026 · by the Qula Craft sales team

Quick answer: Compare bags, bottles, jars and boxes using the same product quantity and retail requirements. Confirm the filled pack, closure, label, packing labor and outer carton before comparing prices. Pack size, MOQ and lead time are separate quotation details; a container name alone does not establish cost, protection or freight savings.

1. Compare formats using your actual product

The options below are starting points for a packaging discussion, not fixed-price or performance specifications. Request prices for the same SKU, net weight or piece count, order quantity and destination. Confirm whether containers, closures, labels, filling, sorting and outer cartons are included.

FormatPossible useDetails to quoteSample check
OPP or resealable bagRefill packs or separated componentsMaterial, thickness, filled dimensions, seal and labelConfirm the closure retains the selected contents and the pack handles as intended
PET bottleSmall portions or display packsContainer dimensions, opening, cap, fill quantity and labelCheck filling and dispensing with the selected slices, beads or sequins

Compartment boxAssortments that need separate sectionsBox dimensions, compartment layout, quantities per section and sortingCheck that the selected contents stay in their intended compartments during handling
Printed gift boxA coordinated kit or presentation packBox structure, insert, artwork, print finish and assemblyApprove an assembled sample with the intended contents and layout

Confirm the material and closure for the exact pack. An OPP bag is not automatically resealable, and nominal bottle or jar capacity does not establish how many grams or pieces of a particular craft material will fit. Use a filled sample to agree the quantity and appearance.

2. Compare freight with packed-carton measurements

Ask for outer carton length, width and height, gross weight, units per carton and total carton count for each proposed format. Compare the same total product quantity. A packaging change can alter both dimensions and weight, but it does not establish a fixed freight multiplier.

DHL Express explains dimensional weight and comparison with actual weight. Its example uses dimensions in centimeters divided by 5,000. Service rules differ: DHL eCommerce's US guidance uses a divisor of 6,000 for centimeter measurements and describes an exception for some domestic packages. Confirm the calculation, rounding and charges for your carrier and service.

Use the agreed ICC Incoterms® rule and named place to clarify delivery responsibilities, costs and risks. Ask the quotation to list what is included. The packaging format alone does not tell you who pays freight or which charges are covered.

3. Match the pack to the sales channel

  • Online refill sales: compare labeled bags with other formats using the filled dimensions, closure, customer handling and total delivered cost.
  • Nail supply distribution: check that the opening and closure work with the selected material; agree net weight or piece count and any display-box arrangement.
  • Slime brand: distinguish bulk inputs for your production from retail topping packs. Confirm the quantity, label and closure for each.
  • DIY kit or subscription box: define the component list, quantities, compartment layout and packing order before approving an assembled sample.
  • Seasonal gifting: plan artwork, samples, packing and transport around your requested arrival date. Use the seasonal planning guide and slime business supply checklist as planning references; confirm the schedule for your order.

Bulk shipment followed by local repacking is another option to cost. Compare any freight difference with local containers, labels, labor, storage and handling. Ask your broker about the product and pack configuration before finalizing the shipment; the International Trade Administration's HS-code overview explains the classification system.

4. Approve branding and environmental wording

For branding, confirm the label or print area, artwork dimensions, application method and approved proof. Discuss the intended sales channel and labeling needs using the labeling guide and private label packaging page. Approve the finished sample before bulk packing.

Do not assume that a PET container or paper box makes the complete package recyclable or compostable. Material, coatings, closures, labels and available facilities matter. The US FTC's environmental-claims guidance explains the evidence and qualifications needed for recycling and composting claims. Confirm support for the exact package and intended market before printing such wording.

5. Send a packaging quotation checklist

  • Product SKU and the intended use or sales channel.
  • Net weight or piece count per pack, assortment and total order quantity.
  • Proposed material, container dimensions, closure and label or print requirements.
  • For kits: components, quantities per section and assembly layout.
  • Required samples, approval steps and any artwork or tooling work.
  • Destination, requested arrival date and shipping terms.

Request an itemized quotation separating product, packaging, filling or sorting, one-time setup, samples and freight where applicable. Confirm MOQ for each pack or design, sample timing, bulk packing lead time and shipping time separately. Mixed pack formats need their own quantities and approval details; availability and timing are confirmed for the project.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask about this topic

Which packaging is cheapest to start with?

Compare itemized quotations for the same product quantity, pack requirements and order volume. Include containers, labels, filling or sorting, outer cartons and freight; a bag or bottle name alone does not establish the lowest total cost.

Why can switching to bottles change my freight quote?

The filled pack and carton layout can change dimensions, gross weight and units per carton. Request those measurements and compare the same shipment quantity using the chosen carrier and service rules; there is no fixed bag-to-bottle freight multiplier.

Can I mix packaging formats in one order?

Send the SKUs, format and quantity for each pack. Availability, MOQ, sample approval, packing cost and timing for mixed formats must be confirmed in the quotation.
